Who am I?

I'm a Senior Technical Product Manager at Unity, based in Dublin, Ireland, where I work on the tools behind games.

Before Unity I spent more than five years at DIGIT, a Scopely studio in Dublin, working on Star Trek Fleet Command. I joined as a Producer and moved through Technical Product Manager, Senior and then Lead Technical Product Manager, looking after all internal tooling for multiple stakeholders and STFC's infrastructure. Before that I was a Technical Producer at Klang Games in Berlin, working with the Backend and AI teams on Seed, and a Game Programming Specialist at Paradox Interactive in Stockholm, an internal technical consultant for the publisher, including on Cities: Skylines for Nintendo Switch. Earlier, I was Lead Game Programmer at Black Shamrock Studios on Blood Bowl and Paranoia.

In my spare time, I love photography, traveling, and play music on percussion and ukulele.
